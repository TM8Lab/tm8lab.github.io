TM8 Lab is an independent hardware engineering business based in the Netherlands. We develop electronic modules for makers, embedded developers, and robotics projects, with attention to compactness, reliable operation, and practical integration.

Our current products include an MMC5983MA compass module and a battery-powered breadboard power supply.
